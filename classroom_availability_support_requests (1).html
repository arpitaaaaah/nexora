Before You Submit

  1. Check the sample room list and choose the room type.
  2. Enter your details and the date and time you need.
  3. Describe the academic purpose of your request.
  4. Submit the form and check the request table below.

Possible support needs include a projector, computer lab access, seating, or help with classroom equipment.
